Research Teaching About Me

I'm currently a continuing lecturer (the UK equivalent of an assistant professor) in the department of philosophy at the University of St Andrews, and a member of the Arché Philosophical Research Centre. Before coming to St Andrews, I was a PhD student at Yale University and a research fellow at the Australian National University.

When I'm not doing philosophy, I like to read and write poetry and fiction. I played the violin until I was in my mid 20s, but these days I'm mostly a listener; I love classical music, metal, hip hop, and a bunch of stuff in between. I also play hockey, Olympic lift, train with kettlebells, and for a long time I ran a small personal training business. These days I mostly just train my friends, if they ask nicely.
